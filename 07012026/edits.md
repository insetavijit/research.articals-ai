# Suggested Edits for Better Readability

As your editor, I recommend the following word and phrase changes to make the story "stick" in the reader's mind. We want to avoid big "corporate" words and keep things simple.

| Original Word / Phrase | Simplified Suggestion | Why it helps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Autonomous AI agents"** | "Smart AI helpers" | Sounds less technical and easier to picture. |
| **"Specialized in"** | "Experts at" | Shorter and more common. |
| **"Total consideration ranging between"** | "Is worth" | Everyone understands "worth." |
| **"Confirmed on December 30"** | "Announced on Dec. 30" | Clearer and more active. |
| **"Unprecedented consolidation"** | "Lots of big buy-outs" | Explains the action in simple terms. |
| **"Strategic repositioning"** | "A new big plan" | Removes "business speak." |
| **"Commercially viable"** | "Ready to make money" | Focuses on the real meaning. |
| **"Multi-agent architecture"** | "A team of smart programs" | Easier to visualize than "architecture." |
| **"Proactive digital assistant"** | "Helper that thinks ahead" | "Proactive" is a complex word. |
| **"Geopolitical complexity"** | "Global politics" | Shorter and clearer. |
| **"Finalization of the deal"** | "Closing the deal" | Standard, simple phrase. |
| **"Scrutiny is almost guaranteed"** | "Folks will be watching closely" | More descriptive and less formal. |
| **"Monetize AI infrastructure"** | "Make money from AI tech" | "Monetize" is jargon. |
| **"Capital expenditure"** | "Spending" | Direct and simple. |
| **"Transactional operating system"** | "A place to buy things" | Focuses on what it actually does for the user. |
| **"Consensus estimates"** | "What experts think" | Simple and relatable. |

---

### General Tips for the Reader:
- **Short Sentences:** Keep thoughts to 10-15 words.
- **Active Voice:** Instead of "The deal was announced," say "Meta announced the deal."
- **Common Words:** If a 5th grader can't understand it, we should simplify it.
