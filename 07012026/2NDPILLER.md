WHAT IS MANUS AI?
================================================================================

Definition:
Manus AI is an autonomous AI agent developed by Monica/Butterfly Effect AI. 
Launched in March 2025, it is designed for high-performance natural language 
processing (NLP), customizable workflows, and real-time data analysis.

Parent Company: Beijing Butterfly Effect Technology (later Monica)
Launch Date: March 2025
Headquarters: Singapore (relocated from China)
Core Technology: General-purpose autonomous AI agent

Key Distinction:
Unlike reactive chatbots that respond to prompts, Manus AI is specifically 
built to plan, execute, and refine multi-step tasks independently. It acts 
like a "digital assistant" or "proactive intern" that can complete complex 
workflows autonomously.
