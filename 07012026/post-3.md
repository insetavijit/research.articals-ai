# Meta Buys Smart AI Startup Manus in Deal Worth up to $3 Billion

**MENLO PARK, Calif.** — Meta Platforms Inc. has announced the buy-out of Manus AI, a Singapore-based startup that are experts at smart AI helpers, for a price ranging between $2 billion and $3 billion. The deal, announced on Dec. 30, marks Meta's biggest move into smart AI tech so far.

Manus AI, started in 2022 as Beijing Butterfly Effect Technology before moving its home to Singapore in 2024, is now bringing in about $125 million a year as of late 2025. The company became famous for its "general AI agent," which works much better and needs less help than other AI tools. The tech uses a team of smart programs that can plan projects, find resources, and use tools on their own—acting like a helper that thinks ahead for big tasks.

The deal includes $500 million to keep employees on board. Meta said it will keep Manus as its own service while also adding the tech to apps like WhatsApp and Instagram. "We see this fitting perfectly with WhatsApp for small businesses," said Barton Crockett, an expert who follows the company.

Global politics have made closing the deal tricky. While Manus moved out of China and closed its offices there, Chinese officials began looking at the deal in January. They want to make sure the tech developed in China wasn't moved to Singapore against the rules.

"Folks will be watching closely," said Jeremy Goldman, an expert on the market. "Any deal with Chinese roots and AI in the name triggers concerns in Washington." The U.S. government had also looked into a $75 million investment in Manus earlier in 2025.

Meta’s boss Mark Zuckerberg has a new big plan for "smart personal AI." He wants to move away from simple chatbots and build tools that can actually finish work for you. Meta plans to use this in WhatsApp for Business, where the AI can handle customer orders from start to finish—basically creating a place to buy things right in the app. This is part of Meta's plan to make money from AI tech after spending over $60 billion on it.

Manus has shown its tech is even better than OpenAI’s tools at solving real-world problems. The company is already ready to make money, reaching $100 million in sales just eight months after launching in March 2025. This makes it a rare success in the AI world.

This buy-out comes after a record year of lots of big buy-outs in the AI world. Total deals hit $157 billion in 2025. Earlier that year, Meta also invested over $14 billion in Scale AI to get more top talent.

As part of the deal, Manus CEO Xiao Hong will join Meta as a Vice President. The company’s top team of about 40 people will also move over. The deal still needs to be cleared by government officials in both the U.S. and China.
