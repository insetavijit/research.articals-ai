# Meta Acquires Agentic AI Startup Manus in Deal Worth up to $3 Billion

**MENLO PARK, Calif.** — Meta Platforms Inc. has announced the acquisition of Manus AI, a Singapore-based startup that specializes in smart AI assistants that can work independently, valued at between **$2 billion and $3 billion**. The transaction, confirmed on December 30, marks the largest investment in agentic AI capabilities by the social media giant to date.

---

### The Technology

Manus AI, founded in 2022 as Beijing Butterfly Effect Technology before relocating to Singapore in 2024, has achieved a revenue run rate of **$125 million** as of late 2025. The company gained attention for its "general AI agent," which operates with far less prompting than existing models.

The technology uses a multi-agent architecture capable of independent planning and tool integration, acting as a proactive "digital assistant" for complex workflows.

The acquisition includes a **$500 million** employee retention pool. Meta will maintain Manus as a standalone service while integrating its core technology across WhatsApp and Instagram. "We see a natural fit into Meta's fast-growing WhatsApp SMB footprint," said Barton Crockett, an analyst at Rosenblatt Securities.

---

### The Global Impact

Global politics have made closing the deal harder. While Manus exited Chinese ownership and closed its Beijing facilities, regulatory concerns have emerged from two directions:

*   **Chinese Scrutiny:** Officials began examining the transaction for potential technology export control violations.
*   **U.S. Scrutiny:** "Anything with Chinese roots and AI in the headline now triggers Washington's reflexes," said Jeremy Goldman, senior director at Emarketer.

---

### Zuckerberg's Future Vision

Meta's CEO Mark Zuckerberg has emphasized a vision for "agentic-rich personal AI," moving away from simple chatbots toward systems that can execute multi-step business tasks.

The integration will focus on the WhatsApp Business API, where autonomous agents could handle end-to-end customer transactions. This shift aims to make money from Meta's projected **$60 billion to $65 billion** spending in AI infrastructure for fiscal 2025.

Commercially, Manus reached **$100 million** in annual recurring revenue within eight months of its March 2025 launch, making it one of the few profitable entities in the early-stage AI sector.

---

### The Bigger Picture

This acquisition follows a record year for AI consolidation, with mergers and acquisitions reaching **$157 billion** across 33 major deals in 2025.

Under the terms of the agreement, Manus CEO Xiao Hong will join Meta as a Vice President. The company's core technical team of about **40 personnel** will also transition. The deal remains subject to regulatory approvals and continued monitoring by U.S. and Chinese authorities.
