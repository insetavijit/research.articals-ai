# Meta Acquires Agentic AI Startup Manus in Deal Valued up to $3 Billion

**MENLO PARK, Calif.** — Meta Platforms Inc. (META.O) has announced the acquisition of Manus AI, a Singapore-based startup that specializes in smart AI assistants that can work independently, valued at between **$2 billion and $3 billion**. The transaction, confirmed on December 30, marks the largest investment in agentic AI capabilities by the social media giant to date.

---

### The Technology

Manus AI, founded in 2022 as Beijing Butterfly Effect Technology before relocating its headquarters to Singapore in 2024, has achieved a revenue run rate of **$125 million** as of late 2025. The company gained international attention for its "general AI agent," which operates with significantly less manual prompting than existing foundational models.

The technology utilizes a multi-agent architecture capable of independent planning, resource allocation, and tool integration, often acting as a proactive "digital assistant" for complex workflows.

The acquisition includes a **$500 million** employee retention pool. Meta indicated it will maintain Manus as a standalone service while integrating its core technology across consumer and business platforms, including WhatsApp and Instagram. "We see a natural fit into Meta's fast-growing WhatsApp SMB footprint," said Barton Crockett, an analyst at Rosenblatt Securities.

---

### The Global Impact

Global politics have made it harder to finish the deal. While Manus restructured its operations to exit Chinese ownership and closed its Beijing facilities, regulatory concerns have emerged from two directions:

*   **Chinese Scrutiny:** Officials in January began examining the transaction for potential technology export control violations, focusing on whether intellectual property developed in China was improperly transferred during the firm's 2024 relocation to Singapore.
*   **U.S. Scrutiny:** "Scrutiny is almost guaranteed," said Jeremy Goldman, senior director at Emarketer. "Anything with Chinese roots and AI in the headline now triggers Washington's reflexes." The U.S. Treasury had previously examined a $75 million Series A investment in Manus led by Silicon Valley firm Benchmark in May 2025.

---

While the politics are complex, Meta's internal focus remains on the technology's potential.

### Zuckerberg's Future Vision

Meta's Chief Executive Mark Zuckerberg has recently emphasized a vision for "agentic-rich personal AI," moving away from simple chatbots toward systems capable of executing multi-step business tasks.

The integration is expected to focus initially on the WhatsApp Business API, where autonomous agents could handle end-to-end customer transactions without human intervention. This shift aims to make money from Meta's projected **$60 billion to $65 billion** spending in AI infrastructure for fiscal 2025.

The startup has demonstrated performance levels exceeding OpenAI's DeepResearch on the Global AI Adoption (GAIA) benchmark. Commercially, Manus reached **$100 million** in annual recurring revenue within eight months of its March 2025 launch, making it one of the few profitable entities in the early-stage AI sector.

---

### The Bigger Picture

This acquisition follows a record year for industry consolidation. Data indicates that AI-related mergers and acquisitions reached **$157 billion** across 33 major deals in 2025. Earlier that year, Meta invested $14.3 billion for a stake in Scale AI, reflecting an intensifying competition for specialized talent.

Under the terms of the agreement, Manus CEO Xiao Hong will join Meta as a Vice President. The company's core technical team of approximately **40 personnel** will also transition. The deal remains subject to standard regulatory approvals and continued monitoring by U.S. and Chinese authorities.
