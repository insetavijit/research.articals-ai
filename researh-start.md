# Research: Meta Acquires Manus AI

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What** | **Meta acquired Manus AI**, a high-profile startup specializing in "Agentic AI"—autonomous models that can execute multi-step tasks like coding and research. The startup was originally founded in China but is now headquartered in Singapore. |
| **Why** | **To win the "Agentic AI" race.** Meta aims to evolve its AI from simple chatbots to functional "digital workers" that can take action. The technology will be integrated into Meta AI across Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp to perform complex tasks for billions of users. |
| **When** | **Announced on December 29, 2025.** The deal was finalized in late 2025, just before the new year, marking a major strategic move to close out the year. |
| **How** | **~$2-3 Billion Deal & "Clean Break" Strategy.** <br>- **Valuation:** Estimated between $2B and $3B.<br>- **Geopolitics:** Meta bought out all Chinese investors (Tencent, Sequoia China, ZhenFund) and Manus will cease all China operations to satisfy U.S. regulatory concerns.<br>- **Structure:** Manus continues as a standalone subscription service while its underlying tech powers Meta's diverse ecosystem. |
| **Who** | **Key Players:**<br>- **Mark Zuckerberg (Meta CEO):** Orchestrated the deal to bolster Meta's long-term AI innovations.<br>- **Xiao Hong (Manus Founder):** Joins Meta as a VP reporting to COO Javier Olivan.<br>- **Javier Olivan (Meta COO):** Will oversee the integration of Manus's leadership.<br>- **Ex-Investors:** Tencent, ZhenFund, and Sequoia China (all bought out). |
